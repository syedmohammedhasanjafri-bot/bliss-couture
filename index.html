Where Thread Meets Reverence

Our Vadodara atelier is not a factory floor. It is a sunlit chamber where twelve master Karigar families — their Adda frames worn smooth by three generations of needlework — translate a bride's whispered vision into silk, gold wire, and Kundan stone. Every lehenga begins with a hand-drawn motif sheet, not a digital pattern file.

01

Zardozi & Dabka

Gold and silver metallic coil thread, twisted and couched by hand onto raw silk. A single 24-Kali panel takes forty days of continuous Adda work.

02

Resham Threadwork

Pure twisted silk thread in jewel-tone hues, hand-stitched into floral Jaal patterns that catch daylight differently from every angle.

03

Kundan Jaal

Uncut polki stones set in gold foil, pressed into Lac beds by hand. Each stone placement follows a hereditary motif map passed through generations.

04

Chandi Zari Border

Real silver wire woven into brocade borders on wooden handlooms. The weight alone distinguishes genuine Chandi Zari from machine-made imitations.

The Karigar Lineage

12
Master Karigar Families

Three-generation embroidery lineages from Lucknow, Bhopal, and Vadodara. Each family carries a hereditary motif vocabulary that cannot be replicated by machine.

3,600
Peak Craft Hours Per Piece

The Sindoor Mahal flagship requires eighteen months of continuous Adda work. No shortcuts. No machine assist. Every stitch is placed, checked, and signed by the lead Karigar.

24
Kali Panels in the Flagship

Each Kali is hand-cut from a single bolt of silk, embroidered individually, then assembled. The 24-Kali ghera creates a fall and movement that no 8-panel lehenga can match.

I

Consultation & Motif Sketching

The bride visits the atelier. Over chai and fabric swatches, the design team hand-draws a motif sheet tailored to her family's regional traditions and personal vision.

II

Fabric Sourcing & Kali Cutting

Raw silk sourced from Varanasi and Mysore. Each Kali panel is cut by a master cutter who has been shaping bridal gheras for twenty-two years.

III

Adda Frame Embroidery

Panels are stretched on the Adda frame. Karigar teams work in shifts — Zardozi and Dabka in daylight, Resham and Kundan under calibrated studio lights.

IV

Assembly, Finishing & Latkan

Embroidered panels are assembled by hand with Chandi Zari borders and gold wire Latkans, then pressed under muslin for delivery.
